# Expeditions: Rome — Class Skills (scraped from wiki)

Source: https://expeditions-viking.fandom.com/wiki/Class_skills_(Rome)

## Tree Structure Rules
- Each class has 3 specializations
- Each specialization = 2 columns × 4 rows = 8 skills
- Tier 1 (row 1): always available
- Tier 2 (row 2): unlocked after investing ≥1 point in the tree
- Tier 3 (row 3): unlocked after investing ≥4 points in the tree
- Tier 4 (row 4): unlocked after investing ≥7 points in the tree
- Max level ~20, meaning ~19 skill points per character

## PRINCEPS

### Defender
**Column 1:**
1. **Brace** (Active, Action) — Regain 2 stacks of strong. 25% Shield strength restored per stack at turn start. One stack lost per hit. Applies deflecting (deflect next melee attack). Upgrade: Regain 4 stacks.
2. **Fortress** (Passive) — Brace restores 1 armour point per stack of strong.
3. **Protect** (Active, Action) — All direct damage to adjacent friendly unit halved and shared with Princeps. Armour/resistance applied separately.
4. **Guardian Angel** (Passive) — Protect now redirects Status effects toward the protecting character.

**Column 2:**
5. **Bastion** (Passive) — +10 to all resistances. Maximum resistance increased from 50% to 60%.
6. **Repair** (Active, 3 Charges, Action) — Applies jury rigged for two turns. Immune to status effects affecting shields. Removes shield-broken, exposed and sundered.
7. **Bulwark** (Passive) — While character has a working shield, counts as cover. Upgrade: counts as high cover.
8. **Shield Wall** (Active, 1 Charge, free action) — Applies Shield Walled and Exhausted, restores Shield Strength to full. Shield walled increases Shield Strength Maximum to 250%; exhausted removes all strong stacks.

### Vanguard
**Column 1:**
1. **Hardened** (Passive) — +10% max health. Upgrade: +20%.
2. **The Best Defense** (Passive) — 20% shield damage at full Shield strength. Upgrade 1: 40%. Upgrade 2: 60%.
3. **Taunt** (Active, Action) — Forces enemy to attack taunting character at start of their turn. Range: 6 hexes.
4. **Bull Rush** (Active, 1 Charge, Action) — Shield bludgeoning damage. Move in straight line, knocking down enemies and allies alike.

**Column 2:**
5. **Frighten** (Active, Action) — -10 morale to all enemies within range. 3 hex radius.
6. **Flawless Defence** (Active, 3 Charges, free action) — Applies defending status, increasing all resistance to highest value. Max resistance +10%, costs 3 movement. Upgrade: +15%.
7. **Adrenaline Junkie** (Passive) — +50% damage when Health < 50% of Maximum Health.
8. **Avenger** (Passive) — When ally incapacitated/killed, Shield Strength restored to full and +20% damage for rest of battle.

### Veteran
**Column 1:**
1. **Nimble** (Passive) — Lowers movement penalty from light or heavier armor by -1. Upgrade: -2.
2. **Headbutt** (Active, 3 Charges, Action) — Unarmed damage. Sets attacker movement to 0. Applies Weakened. If target already Weakened/Harried: Stunned instead.
3. **Battering Ram** (Passive) — +1 base damage to equipped shields in all conditions. Upgrade: +3 total.
4. **War Cry** (Active, 1 Charge, Action) — Applies stunned to enemies in 1 hex radius. Upgrade: 2 hex radius.

**Column 2:**
5. **Knockdown** (Active, 2 Charges, Action) — Unarmed damage. Applies knocked down.
6. **Opportunist** (Passive) — Attacks of opportunity apply Weakened. Upgrade: applies Stunned.
7. **Stand Fast** (Active, Action) — Allies in 2 hex radius immune to crippled/stunned/stuck/knocked down for 2 turns. Also removes those effects.
8. **Immoveable** (Passive) — Immunity to being moved by rebuke, shove, Shield Push, Push and Shove, Repulse, Counter-Advance.

## SAGITTARIUS

### Marksman
**Column 1:**
1. **Interrupt** (Active, Action) — Attacks next target that moves in 120° cone in front. Slightly more damage. Range 12 hexes. Upgrade: 160° cone.
2. **Vigilant** (Passive) — Interrupt hits up to 2 targets instead of 1.
3. **Gracing shot** (Active) — Applies gracing to self. Next ranged attacks deal slashing damage with -33% accuracy and shred 1 armour. Upgrade: shred 2.
4. **Steady Hands** (Passive) — 100% critical chance for all ranged reaction attacks (Interrupt and Overwatch).

**Column 2:**
5. **Point Blank** (Passive) — 100% critical chance for ranged attacks against adjacent opponents. Provokes Attack of Opportunity.
6. **Rebuke** (Active, Action) — 3 bludgeoning damage. Pushes target 1 hex away. Usable on friendly characters. Upgrade: doubles damage.
7. **Powerful Kick** (Passive) — Rebuke also applies knocked down. Upgrade: applies Tactical effect to archer.
8. **Overwatch** (Active, 1 Charge, Action) — Shoots each enemy that attacks an ally within range (once per enemy). Weapon damage, 6 hex radius. Deflected by shield.

### Hunter
**Column 1:**
1. **Quick Shot** (Active, Action) — Shoots 2 arrows at 2 targets with -30% accuracy, 30% chance to miss. Weapon damage, deflected by shields.
2. **Lure** (Active, 2 Charges, free action) — Bludgeoning damage. Prevents target from using cover (lured effect). Range 6 hexes.
3. **Pinning shots** (Passive) — All ranged hits within 2 hexes apply crippled. Upgrade 1: range 4 hexes. Upgrade 2: applies Stuck instead.
4. **Barrage** (Active, 1 Charge, free action) — On each shot, regain action point. -33% accuracy. Lasts 1 turn only.

**Column 2:**
5. **Versatile** (Passive) — After any attack, next attack of the other type (melee↔ranged) deals +50% damage. Upgrade: also +1 armour penetrating.
6. **Skirmisher** (Passive) — Versatile has 100% critical chance when previous attack killed a target.
7. **Arrow Stab** (Active, Action) — Weapon damage at half damage, inflicts bleeding. When flanked, target also stunned.
8. **Find weakness** (Passive) — Grants Finding Weakness each turn. Next attack inflicts Bleeding, Weakened, or Harried.

### Sniper
**Column 1:**
1. **Ranging shot** (Active, 3 Charges, free action) — Range 4 hexes. Applies spotted in 2 hex radius (spotted = full damage regardless of distance). Finds invisible enemies, breaks invisibility for 2 turns.
2. **Spotter** (Passive) — Ranging shots now shoots two arrows at chosen spots.
3. **Eagle Eye** (Active, 2 Charges, free action) — Next ranged attack ignores target's shield strength and cannot be Resisted.
4. **Seize ground** (Passive) — Start combat with double movement for first 2 turns. Killing target >12 hexes away replenishes a random skill charge.

**Column 2:**
5. **Marksman** (Passive) — +2 accuracy (range). Upgrade: +4.
6. **Mark target** (Active, Action) — Applies marked to target up to 10 hexes. Target can be attacked at any range. Next ranged attack deals +25% damage; if killed by ranged attack, effect jumps to next target. Upgrade: +50%.
7. **Walk Your Shots** (Passive) — +2 optimal range (not max range). Upgrade 1: another +2. Upgrade 2: +6 total.
8. **Assist** (Active, 3 Charges, Action) — Weapon damage. Target provokes attack of opportunity as if they moved. Nearby allies may attack.

## TRIARIUS

### Medic
**Column 1:**
1. **Field Medic** (Passive) — +25% health restored when healing. Upgrade: +50%.
2. **Cure** (Active, 2 Charges, free action) — Removes bleeding, burned, burning, and poisoned.
3. **Good as New** (Passive) — Cure also restores 25% health per status effect removed.
4. **Interference** (Passive) — When any enemy in reach attacks an ally, this character may perform an attack of opportunity.

**Column 2:**
5. **Logistics** (Active, Action) — +2 movement to allies in 3 hex radius for 2 turns. Upgrade: +3 movement, 4 hex radius.
6. **Poisoner** (Passive) — 5% chance any attack inflicts Poison. Upgrade: 10%.
7. **Revive** (Active, 3 Charges, Action) — Bring incapacitated ally back with 25% health. Revived character can move, no action point. Provokes attack of opportunity. Upgrade: 50% health.
8. **Curse** (Active, 1 Charge, Action) — Apply Weakened to multiple enemies in 6 hex radius.

### Flagbearer
**Column 1:**
1. **War Horn** (Active, 3 Charges, Action) — +20% damage to allies for 2 turns in 3 hex radius. Upgrade: 3 turns, 4 hex radius.
2. **Guidance** (Passive) — Downing enemy grants +3 morale to allies within 3 hexes. Upgrade: +5.
3. **Ruse** (Active, 2 Charges, Action) — All enemies in area move out. Range 8 hexes, 1 hex radius (7 hexes total).
4. **Embarrassing ruse** (Passive) — Ruse also lowers morale by -10 for enemies in hexes. Upgrade: -20.

**Column 2:**
5. **Bolstering presence** (Passive) — Grants bolstered to all adjacent allies, allowing them to not fail morale checks.
6. **Inspire** (Active, 3 Charges, Action) — Grants an ally who spent their action point a new one. Provokes attack of opportunity. Range 8 hexes.
7. **Orator** (Passive) — Inspire also resets cooldown of skills of the ally.
8. **Rousing speech** (Active, 1 Charge, Action) — Restore 15 health or 25% total health of allies in 6 hex radius. Upgrade: 2 charges.

### Destroyer
**Column 1:**
1. **Deathblow** (Active, Action) — +50% damage against stunned, knocked down, and sundered characters. Upgrade: also applies to stuck and crippled.
2. **Shredder** (Passive) — +1 armor shredding for staves and pikes.
3. **Reckless** (Active, 2 Charges, free action) — +25% base damage and +1 armour shredding for 2 turns. No resistances, cannot use tactical weapons. Upgrade: +50% base damage.
4. **Sunder** (Active, 2 Charges, Action) — Sets shield strength to 0 before dealing damage. Cannot be resisted (except hunkering down). Applies sundering effect.

**Column 2:**
5. **Fortune favoured** (Passive) — +5% critical hit chance. Upgrade: +10%.
6. **Quick lunge** (Active, 3 Charges, free action) — Half damage weapon attack, cripples enemies, no armour shred. Range 1-2 hexes.
7. **Quick feet** (Passive) — No longer triggers attacks of opportunity when moving around enemies (only when disengaging).
8. **Finisher** (Passive) — Attacking stuck/knocked down/stunned enemy restores 10% health. Killing them restores action point and resets Deathblow. Upgrade: 20% health.

## VELES

### Assassin
**Column 1:**
1. **Cheap shot** (Active, costs 2 Focus, free action) — Applies Savage to self. Next attack is a critical hit.
2. **Assassinate** (Passive) — +40% damage against enemy leaders (characters with laurels in portrait).
3. **Focus Master** (Passive) — +1 Focus Maximum. Upgrade: +2 total.
4. **Rush** (Active, 2 Charges, costs 6 Focus, free action) — Restore action point. Upgrade: 5 focus cost, 3 charges.

**Column 2:**
5. **Lone Wolf** (Passive) — Critical chance doubled and +20% dodge when all allies are ≥6 hexes away.
6. **Sneak Attack** (Active, Action) — Off-hand weapon/Unarmed damage. Generates 1 focus. +50% damage first 2 turns. Upgrade: +100%.
7. **Marathon** (Active, 1 Charge, free action) — Applies inexhaustible: unlimited movement until character stops.
8. **First strike** (Passive) — +50% damage against targets with full health.

### Duelist
**Column 1:**
1. **Shiv** (Active, costs 3 Focus, free action) — Quick off-hand attack at half damage, no action point required.
2. **Bloodthirsty** (Passive) — Killing enemy grants 1 stack of Bloodthirsty (+5% damage). Upgrade: +15% per stack. Max 2 stacks = 30% extra total.
3. **Kill on Ground** (Active, 3 Charges, costs 2 Focus, free action) — Finish off incapacitated enemy, inflict -10 morale on nearby enemies in 5 hex radius.
4. **Quick-witted** (Passive) — +1 focus gained per attack.

**Column 2:**
5. **Born ready** (Passive) — Start with 4 focus points. Upgrade 1: 8. Upgrade 2: 12.
6. **Dodge** (Active, costs 4 Focus) — Next attack has 50% chance to miss; if dodged, no damage or status effects.
7. **Slippery** (Passive) — Dodge lasts until beginning of character's next turn (instead of ending after first attack).
8. **Duel** (Active, 1 Charge, 1 Action + 2 Focus) — Calls out specific enemy; next attack deals double damage, inflicts stunned and bleeding. Range 12 hexes.

### Brawler
**Column 1:**
1. **Tactical advance** (Active, costs 2 Focus, free action) — No attacks of opportunity while moving. Upgrade: +2 movement and activates traps.
2. **Clear Path** (Passive) — On killing target, replenish 2 movement to nearby allies and self. 2 hex radius.
3. **Pugilist** (Passive) — +1 focus per attack when using unarmed skills.
4. **Reaper** (Active, 1 Charge, costs 4 Focus, free action) — While active, killing an enemy restores action point, resets skills, and restores movement.

**Column 2:**
5. **Pankration** (Passive) — +100% unarmed damage.
6. **Feint** (Active, requires 1 movement, free action) — Move through hex occupied by enemy, ending in hex behind them. Usable on friendly characters too.
7. **Boast** (Active, costs 2 Focus, free action) — On killing enemy, allies gain +10 morale; on failure, all enemies gain +10 morale. Upgrade: grants Savage on success.
8. **Heavy skirmisher** (Passive) — Rank 1: can wear strong armour. Rank 2: -1 movement for all body armour.
