Requirements for each node (both needed):
- Any one other node that 'unlocks it'. 
- Previous upgrade for that building also needs to be built to unlock. (each building has 3 tiers, first tier does not need this requirement, only path) 

Outpost buildings are as follows, they should have same icons to indicate that they're linked:
R1 -> 6 -> 22 (medical, plus icon)
R2 -> 18 -> 12 (market, coins icon)
R3 -> 16 -> 23 (recruitment, tent icon)
1 -> 7 -> 8 (tactical items, grenade icon)
14 -> 21 -> 13 (blacksmith, anvil and hammer icon)
2 -> 4 -> 20 (morale, roman aqueduct icon)
5 -> 19 -> 11 (defence, brick wall icon)
15 -> 10 -> 24 (stratagems, cards icon)
17 -> 3 -> 9 (offence, storage shelves icon)

R1 - Dispensary - Leave injured Praetorians here to treat them for free. Critical injuries can only be treated in the dispensary. Upgrade to decrease treatment time. - Unlocks 1. 
R2 - Market - Trade resources for other resources. Upgrade to unlock a wider variety of wares and gain better exchange rates. - Unlocks nothing.
R3 - Barracks - Leave a Praetorian here to refresh the available recruits. Available recruits will be between level 1 and 6. Upgrade to increase the level of the recruits to 7-12. - Unlocks 14.
